Meet The O'Bryan Team

At College Park Realty, we pride ourselves on matching clients with exceptional representation. Michelle O’Bryan is a California-licensed real estate salesperson who has represented buyers and sellers in residential transactions across Southern California, with additional documented sales in the Sacramento region.
